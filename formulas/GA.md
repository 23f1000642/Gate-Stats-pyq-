## Verbal ability
- **Prepositions to remember:** conferred **on**, owe **to**, event **at** a venue. Correlatives: neither…nor, either…or.
- **Agreement:** "X along with / as well as Y" takes X's number ("The President, along with…, **wishes**").
- **Verb forms:** wake–woke–woken. Past perfect passive: "had been defeated". Modal of possibility: "might".
- **Contrast words** (despite, although, yet) flip the expected sense of the blank.
- **Analogies:** test the relation (opposite, degree, time direction) in both pairs. Intensity scales: bothered → fazed → daunted.

## Reading & inference
- Choose what the passage **supports**. Reject options with "always / whenever / only / no other".
- "Find the FALSE statement": look for an option that **contradicts** the passage, not one that is merely unstated.
- **Sentence ordering:** opener (setting or time) → cause → effect → "Thus/Hence" conclusion.

## Logical reasoning
- "Some A are not B" means "some non-B are A". "Some A are B" means "some B are A". "All A are B" does **not** reverse.
- A complementary pair of conclusions ("some are" / "none are") gives **either I or II**.
- **Truth-teller puzzles:** start from the person known to be truthful. A self-referential "all X lie" said by an X must be false.
- **Seating:** place fixed opposite/adjacent pairs first and test "must be true" against **every** valid arrangement.
- **Series:** check first differences, then second differences.

## Quantitative aptitude
- Equal SP with $x\%$ gain and $x\%$ loss gives a net loss of $\frac{x^2}{100}\%$.
- **Scaling:** each of $k$ factors up $p\%$ gives $(1+p)^k$. Cone volume $\frac13\pi r^2h$.
- **Compound growth backwards:** $\frac{P}{(1+r)^n}$.
- $x^2+\frac1{x^2}=\left(x+\frac1x\right)^2-2$. AM = GM iff all values are equal.
- Sum $1..m=\frac{m(m+1)}2$. Sum of the first $n$ odd numbers $=n^2$.
- **Alligation:** cheap : dear $=(\text{dear}-\text{mean}):(\text{mean}-\text{cheap})$.
- $|A\cup B|=|A|+|B|-|A\cap B|$.
- **Relative speed:** opposite directions add, same direction subtract.
- $\log_xy=\frac{\ln y}{\ln x}$.

## Geometry & data interpretation
- Regular hexagon: interior angle $120°$. Sector area $=\frac\theta{360}\pi r^2$.
- **Heron:** $\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$. The 13-14-15 triangle has area 84. Altitude $=\frac{2\cdot\text{Area}}{\text{base}}$.
- Kite or rhombus area $=\frac12d_1d_2$. 3-4-5 right triangle altitude to hypotenuse $=2.4$.
- Similar figures: area ratio $=$ (side ratio)$^2$.
- **Symmetry:** mirror every shaded cell across each axis and count what is missing.
- **Counting squares** in an $n\times n$ grid: $\sum k^2$, minus those containing holes.
- **Charts:** compute percentage change on the base year, and read stacked or pie shares as parts of 100.
